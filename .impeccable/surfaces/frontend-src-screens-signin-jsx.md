# Sign-in surface

Scope: the sign-in and create-account page (`frontend/src/screens/SignIn.jsx`). Visitor mode: Persuade. The page has its own world, separate from the Highlighted Textbook app behind it (owner's choice, 2026-11-06: "New look for this page", "Pure fun and brand"). Fonts stay Fraunces and Karla.

Audience: students, professors and employees arriving at guruCool for the first time or coming back. Job: sign in or create an account, and remember the brand. Constraints: the whole page fits one viewport without scrolling at common desktop and phone sizes, and covers the full page width; no emojis, em dashes or en dashes in copy.

Memorable moment: the guru notices you. He peers over his shades at what you type, covers his eyes for your password, and his halo fills as your new password grows long enough.

## Direction contract

THESIS: guruCool taken at its word: a cool guru in sunglasses floats on a poster ground and watches you sign in. Refuses the split screen of a form beside a product screenshot, and the centred card on a gradient.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched rani pink ground edge to edge, also behind the page so overscroll stays pink. A sun-yellow disc behind his head and four halo rings, lit alternately saffron and sun-yellow; an unlit ring is a deeper rani pink, its own designed state (adapted from 'alternating pink rings': lit rings read as a halo, pink-on-pink did not). Guru in a saffron robe in flat shapes, deep indigo line with a heavier silhouette than interior lines, indigo shades. Indigo text, white field slabs with a 2.5px indigo border; the email field holds an indigo seal that pops in when the address is complete, the password field holds four beads that fill with the halo. A sun-yellow primary button with an indigo edge. Fraunces at display scale for the name, Karla for every control.

STORY: The visitor sees a character, reads that guruCool answers from their own PDF, and signs in or creates an account in two fields while the guru reacts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55 percent, the guru and his halo fill the full height, the outer ring running clearly past the left and top edges; his speech bubble sits just above his head with its tail pointing at him (moved from the right column so the line is plainly his). Right 45 percent, the guruCool name at display size, one line of what it does, the sign-in or create switch, two fields and the button. Below 820px wide the guru is framed close on his head and shoulders above the form so his face stays readable. Primary action sits inside the first viewport at 1280 by 720, 390 by 844 and 375 by 667.

FORM: The Cool Guru, candidate 7 of the re-rolled grounded list (the literal reading of the name), raised with: one hidden mark (the guru reads a guruCool page), streamed greeting, designed unlit halo rings, one saturated note at a time, and one sweeping arc (the halo) ruling the left half. seed:58fe8b1c1a4498d5
